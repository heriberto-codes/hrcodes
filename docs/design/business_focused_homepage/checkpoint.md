## Resolved decisions

- The proposed hierarchy supports the business and visitor goals by guiding prospective clients from problem recognition through services, evidence, process, authority, founder trust, and a qualified project inquiry.
- The retained direction set is acceptable and selected as the approved design.
- Insights Article B appears only after the featured Guardrails article is opened from Insights C. It does not appear inline on the homepage, and the other Insights choices continue to their respective article pages.
- Preserve the current selected visual and content direction; no design revisions were requested during direction selection.
- The documented desktop and mobile behaviors are acceptable, including responsive stacking, full-width mobile actions, native mobile navigation, and reduced-motion fallbacks.
- The named direction set is explicitly approved for `turtle-plan`. This approval authorizes planning only, not implementation.

## Requested revisions

None.

## Accepted tradeoffs

- The expressive, motion-rich design will require performance checks and restrained fallbacks on lower-powered devices.
- Interactive disclosures, tabs, the Insights tuner, the skillset visualization, and the three-step brief builder require progressive enhancement and accessibility verification.
- The complete buyer journey is intentionally substantial in length, while repeated in-page actions provide shorter conversion paths.
- The embedded build video adds third-party page weight and privacy considerations.
- Insights Article B is a tailored experience for the featured Guardrails article rather than an inline homepage section or an automatically approved template for every article.
